about

Hi, I'm Ida!

I work across the fields of data journalism, design and science.

I try to make complex topics more tangible and easy or interesting to engage with. I code, design, build things, do research, file requests and scrape data. I work freelance and was part of the Institute for Remote Sensing and Earth System Science at Uni Leipzig. Sometimes I teach or do workshops, too.
